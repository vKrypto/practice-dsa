leadership principles:
**Respect the Individual:** Treat every associate, customer, and team member fairly. Example: a manager listens to a cashier’s idea instead of ignoring them because of their role.

**Act with Integrity:** Be honest and do the right thing. Example: if a billing mistake happens, the employee corrects it instead of hiding it.

**Serve Customers and Members:** Put customer needs first. Example: helping a customer find the right product instead of just saying “check aisle 5.”

**Strive for Excellence:** Keep improving work quality and speed. Example: if checkout lines are always long, the team improves staffing or opens more counters during peak hours.
